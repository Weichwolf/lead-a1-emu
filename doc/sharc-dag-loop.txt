DAG-load and single-counter-loop composition

PGRrev2.4,4-110..113/Table4-52 permits an unrelated DO to advance while its
dependent RAM body remains before Decode. The single-body rules in4-57,
4-61..63/Tables4-17..20 retain the body, redirect at DO Address and introduce
a distinct startup hold. Pages4-118/119 warn that stalls need not merge.
This composition is a reference interpretation of these rules, tested against
independent instruction/data expectations and original execution. It is not
a manufacturer-provided combined waveform, simulator measurement or physical
silicon timing qualification.

During the qualified DAG hold, immediate single-body DO reaches Address and
retains the already fetched raw F2 body. The pending loop cursor redirects
without accepting a new F1; existing F1/F2 words remain held. Body operands
are captured only upon actual Decode entry, after the real DAG producer
retires. DO itself executes normally. Its separate startup hold remains.
When no body copy has issued yet, the loop fetch counter waits for that first
issue. Existing stages and counts retain this state; no hidden timer,
synthetic instruction, host memory clearing or guessed register value is used.

Typed restore validation covers the zero-issued hold/seed shapes and first-
issued stream shape, including actual Decode/Address words and issued-minus-
retired latches. Zero-issued Decode must match the retained body opcode.
Native DO/loop retirement, actual frame push/pop, data operations and DAG
post-modifies still perform all architectural changes. Failed quiet controller
ownership or a body store with pending pre-pop preserves the staged boundary;
earlier committed stores remain committed and repair resumes the captured word.

The existing count1/longer-loop, live-loop dependency, branch, PM/cache,
pending/in-flight MODE1, width and unsupported body/producer guards remain.
This extends only the qualified entry overlap, not arbitrary merging of stalls.

Owned tests contain a literal13-column composed waveform,80 finite cases
across I/B/L/M producers, counts2/3/4/5/16, primary/secondary banks and register/
immediate modifiers. They check real addresses, payloads, iteration counts,
tail suppression and native continuation/stack state. An unrelated M load
does not acquire a false immediate-modifier dependency. Eight independently
rebound RAM/controller/CPU/transport continuations cover before/after Execute
around delayed setup; actual instruction-RAM replacement gives two or three
retained old body copies followed by refetched replacements. Quiet clock-owner
repair, failed actual store/pre-pop repair and malformed Decode snapshots have
separate checks. No manufacturer/private instructions are public test inputs.

Each of eight optional original-stream invocations also owns a fresh sequenced
RAM/PMCTL initialization fixture. After six PLL waits and the first48768 stores,
the unchanged original executes all three remaining clear loops:49151/32767/
32767 actual stores, totaling163453 stores over all four loops. All cleared
words are checked; the three excluded final normal words remain
unknown. All384 kernel words survive, and the actual RTS pops its original
CALL frame and returns to8c008 with depth0 and I0=e7fff. No host initialization
or loop summary substitutes for these operations.

The RAM-clear prefix retires171758 instructions using171776 AVAILABLE-BUS grants.
Its earlier first-clear checkpoint remains57065/57077 at8c0b9. Code/data block
arbitration is still not coupled to these granted fetch clocks; they are not
physical CCLK, MHz or a board schedule. Direct serial record-stream fixtures
remain separate and stop at8c08e. No original terminal-vector acceptance,
complete uploader/application boot, MIDI/audio/update/recovery or hardware
comparison follows. Those remain part of the full objective.
