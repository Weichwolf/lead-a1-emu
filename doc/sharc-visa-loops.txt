Clocked single-instruction VISA counter loops

PGRrev2.4 4-54/55 requires uncompressed48-bit short-loop bodies. The bound
transport now supports an immediate DO with one such body and count>=2.
Loop metadata explicitly retains address units1(native NW) or3(VISA SW).
Body/end/post-loop addresses and real PC frames use those units. The VISA
entry currently requires a complete aligned body burst and no delayed burst;
partial/mixed entry and count1 remain explicit emulator gaps.

The actual Decode body is retained across DO/startup holds. Overridden or
aborted Decode inputs discard their captured burst without semantic parsing;
they are not executable NOPs. A real nonsequential F2 body response starts a
new IAB epoch using three actual executable SW reads. Subsequent Decode uses
the newly captured bits. RAM changes therefore preserve already locked body
copies while affecting later refetches. The normal IAB resumes for compact
post-loop code, including fetch backpressure and immediate redirects.

Tables4-17..20 determine startup, F1 counter checks, early pop and drain.
Counts>=4 terminate at the documented F1 threshold4; counts2/3 retain their
three aborted tail clocks. PC/loop stack pop precedes final body retirement.
A taken immediate branch immediately after a VISA loop may select its target
while the last body executes, only after the loop frame has popped. Metadata
survives until the remaining body instructions/buffered responses drain.

Owned literal short-word phase/pop tables cover counts2,3,4,5. Further checks
exercise changed RAM versus retained/refetched bodies, compact tails, real
CALL frames/RTS followed by reserved wrong-path bytes, restored held state,
malformed spans and rejected count1/compact body entry. Address bounds are
checked before addition, including malformed native UINT_MAX state. Existing
native loop and VISA fetch regressions remain independent expectations.

The candidate original profiles continue from the actual JUMP/first CALL
into common entry1c0528. Its8284 further instructions take8293 further grants:
three refill clocks and six real loop holds. Six observed NOP counts remain
16/4096/16/4096/16/16, with11 actual PMCTL accesses and the actual compact RTS
back to entry+3. The instruction-order reference receives its checkpoint only
after the native F2/Decode cache fill has completed; it cannot deliver that
transport input itself. The resulting complete CPU states, including cache,
are compared. No firmware instruction, PC or readback is substituted.

The selected loader response and CLKIN reference remain explicit fixture
inputs. These clocks do not qualify physical Nord timing or a complete boot.
Count1, partial/mixed loop entry, register/multi-body/arithmetic loops and
PM/cache/IRQ compositions still need their actual paths implemented. Original
firmware is known to work; these are emulator gaps, not accepted limitations.
Full instrument/audio/MIDI/USB/UI/storage/update/recovery/hardware acceptance
remains mandatory and incomplete.
