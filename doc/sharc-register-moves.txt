Native SHARC universal-register transfers

The compute-free Type5a SISD UREG transfer uses PGR rev2.4 9-22..24 and the
actual field diagram10-9, with register codes in10-32. Source bits6:2 reside
in native bits42:38 and source bits1:0 in32:31. Destination bits6:0 reside
in29:23. Bit30 is reserved, not another source bit. COND is37:33. Swap and
nonzero parallel COMPUTE fields remain guarded; SIMD transfer is rejected
before a false PEx condition could hide an implicit PEy operation.

A false SISD condition reads no source or destination bank. The instruction
captures the source before writing its destination through the existing UREG
interfaces. Register knowledge is preserved, not replaced by guessed values.
The existing effective MODE1 selects data/DAG banks, and Bx writes still load
Ix. SREG destinations retain their existing write/latency/readonly contracts;
unimplemented selectors fail with native PC/opcode context. Transfers do not
implicitly compute or change arithmetic flags. Native count-loop bodies do
not yet accept this transfer class.

Data-file-to-DAG/SREG and DAG/SREG-to-data-file moves transfer upper32 bits,
aligned at bits39:8 of the data register. Such data-register writes clear the
low8 extension bits. Data-to-data file copies remain guarded until their
separate full-width transfer behavior is qualified; they are not silently
truncated. Explicit Sx upper32 reads in SISD use the PEy register bank.
Combined-PX/data40 and split-PX/UREG32 moves have their separate known-bit
contract in sharc-px.txt. Swap, SIMD pairing and parallel compute remain guarded.
Primary alignment references are2-5,2-10,6-5/Figure6-3.

sharc_register_moves uses literal encodings for data/B/I/USTAT/explicit-Sx
transfers, split source fields, partial knowledge, Bx-to-Ix side effects,
zeroed extension bits, preserved flags, false conditions/unknown banks,
secondary DAG selection, MODE1 effect latency and failed-mode/field rollback.
The actual kernel I0=R3 and USTAT2=R2 execute in all four original placements
without a special PC/opcode callback (sharc-arithmetic.txt).
