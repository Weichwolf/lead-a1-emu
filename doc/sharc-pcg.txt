Precision clock generator source-edge reference

SharcPcg owns PCG A..D at native24c0..24cb: CTLA0/A1/B0/B1/PW1/SYNC1,
then CTLC0/C1/D0/D1/PW2/SYNC2. HWR15-4..22,A-189..197,B-19 establish
this family reference; reference/sharc.json pins the manual. Raw reset words
are zero, but disabled output voltages and source levels remain UNKNOWN.
Register reset is not a measured Nord reset or package/board qualification.

observe() supplies actual optional source levels. CLKIN, PCLK and four EXT
inputs are distinct; SYNC clock/frame PCLK overrides beat CTL1's independent
EXT/CLKIN selections. A write/read, CCLK advance or unrelated input cannot
create a source edge. advance_half_periods() supplies elapsed SOURCE time
only for disable settling; it never toggles a source or advances a divider.
The DAI clock gate covers all four PCGs, including C/D with DPI outputs.

Clock DIV0/1 bypasses; DIV2..fffff counts positive source edges with HIGH
floor(DIV/2), LOW ceil(DIV/2). Bypass starts at the first known positive
edge; divided clocks at the third. This edge-count reference does not qualify
absolute PCLK/enable phase, analog propagation or Nord frequencies. Normal
frame DIV>1 supports a16-bit pulse width, zero meaning floor(DIV/2), and a
20-bit phase assembled from two10-bit fields excluding enable/source bits.
The synchronous pair uses one atomic enable, the same source and an integral
frame/clock divisor ratio for nonzero phase. Phase leads modulo the frame
period. The logical counter shares the synchronous clock start reference;
a possibly truncated first nonzero-phase frame stays UNKNOWN until its first
complete frame. Initial truncated waveform and silicon frame-start timing
remain unqualified. DIV0/1 frame bypass supports inversion. External trigger
and one-shot strobe generation explicitly reject pending their timing models.

Missing source history loses divided phase; subsequent known levels do not
restart a counter or invent elapsed edges. Disable/rearm with known history
is required to recover. Changing parameters while enabled or stopping rejects;
a full supplied source period must follow disable before reconfiguration.
Paired enables require paired atomic disable. Snapshots preserve raw registers,
source knowledge, startup, counter/full-frame phase, disable waits and pairing.
Malformed state rejects. Register transactions stage effects; source/elapsed
samples and snapshots reject during an active instruction. Unmapped/misaligned/
wrong-width/oversized accesses reject. The IOP bridge adds PC/address/context.

Owned tests use72 literal unit/source/clock-divider cases, even/odd frame
patterns, pulse1/8, bypass/inversion, independent clock/frame sources and both

not a licensed ADI simulator or physical instrument comparison.
