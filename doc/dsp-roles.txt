DSP application roles: static evidence and unresolved routing

The two main-image DSP streams contain substantial shared synthesis, control,
filter and effect code. Their logical transport numbers are the original ARM
uploader arguments, not an established physical package order or slot assignment.
The two bundle parts must also remain separate from these transport indices.

The private reconstruction's factual main-frame-engine, main-table-calls,
main-filters-and-delay and main-effects contracts identify the following
unchanged OS1.36 structures. These are static instruction/dataflow observations,
not sound output, physical measurements or imported implementation:
- Both programs prepare modulation/control records and phase callbacks, render
  and filter sums, and invoke selected effect/mix and feedback-network callbacks.
- The shared main filter range is DSP short-word addresses1c3e8a..1c47d0.
  Equivalent instruction schedules use different explicit state/table bindings.
- The four-iteration audio loop begins at1c572e for transport0 and1c5733 for1.
  Four loop iterations do not establish four user slots, voices or SIMD lanes.
- Transport0 replaces four frame words with scaled sums. Transport1 adds its
  scaled sums to existing words. Both then retain effect/mix processing.
- Transport0's final path contains saturation, CLIP/FIX conversion and
  accumulator operations. Transport1 copies work words to its frame cursor
  without that final conversion path. This does not identify codec/sample format.

The evidence supports distributed synthesis with different audio-routing roles;
it does not support assigning one chip exclusively to synthesis and the other
exclusively to effects. Two slots per chip remains a hypothesis. Slot A-D and
voice ownership, physical SPORT directions/interconnect, sample rate, block
timing, silicon revision and the binding of either stream to a photographed
package need execution and hardware evidence. Callback/record counts alone
must not become a host voice-allocation policy.

Preserve both original programs and their causal exchanges in the emulator.
Choose host SIMD packing or concurrency from observed workloads after exact
reference arithmetic and inter-processor scheduling exist. AVX512 availability
does not establish safe four-slot batching or independent physical DSP clocks.
