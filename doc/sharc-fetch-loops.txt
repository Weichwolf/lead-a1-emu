Single-instruction counter loops in the bound native fetch sequencer

The separate five-or-more-instruction immediate/UREG path and original first
SPI header/payload continuation are described in sharc-fetch-long-loops.txt.

PGRrev2.4 4-59..63, Tables4-17..20, describe the qualified immediate-DO cases:
counts2/3/4/5 and larger, with encoded zero meaning2^32 iterations. The body is
one native NOP, supported compute-only instruction, immediate shifter
instruction, or the bounded DM RAM forms in sharc-dag-operands.txt. The actual decoder executes every retained body word; there is no
host wait, repeated functional-step timing summary or opcode patch. The default
instruction-only loop helper retains its separate lower bounds.

Address selects the loop F1 cursor and retains the original Decode body word.
After native DO executes, one partial startup stall holds F1/F2/Decode while the
first real body advances to Execute. A subsequent seed clock retains Decode once
more, so up to three initial body executions use the captured word. Later copies
come from actual F2 RAM/cache reads. DMA/self-modification can therefore change
later iterations without changing the held initial copies. The held Decode arrow
in the tables is represented by a captured word plus an issue stall; an aborted
copy is absent, never an executable inserted NOP.

A fetch counter selects the continuation at the documented termination test:
four remaining for counts>=4, one for counts2/3. This is transport bookkeeping,
not guest-visible LCNTR/CURLCNTR knowledge. Counts2/3 discard the three excess
body copies and retain the resulting three absent Execute words. Count>=4 has
the single startup bubble and no final bubble. Suppressed copies do not execute
or decrement the architectural instruction-retirement count.

The native loop PC frame pops one clock after the fetch termination test. For
count4 that clock has absent Execute: the CPU copy changes only when the whole
clock commits. Otherwise the real body instruction performs the pop inside its
ordinary instruction transaction. Foreign controller ownership rejects a quiet
pop before committing the staged CPU/transport. Failed arithmetic/operands keep
the occupied Execute word, live frame and pending pop for retry. A late F2
failure keeps the entire CPU/cache/transport clock unchanged. Parent CALL frames
survive. No independent shadow architectural PC stack is introduced.

Typed CPU/transport snapshots retain startup phase, original Decode word,
fetch/issued/retired counts and pending pop. Coupled guards require matching
native loop/count/stack state. Metadata drains only after the final real body
has retired and all remaining body copies have left the transport. Address
bounds precede continuation addition, including corrupt32-bit snapshot inputs.

This is a bounded pipeline reference. UREG-count DO, count1, longer bodies,
Other RAM/DAG/PM loop bodies, nested/delayed DO, loop-resource operations, combined
cache/control stalls, MODE1 hazards and interrupt/IDLE redirects stay guarded.
The further qualified DAG-load overlap at single-loop entry has the
sharc-dag-loop.txt contract; its zero-issued startup states retain actual words.
Count1 Table4-21's F1 row and note4 disagree about the continuation clock; that
discrepancy is not resolved by the instruction-only helper's aggregate cycle
lower bound. Physical Nord clocks and complete Decode operand capture remain
open. No original application boot or audio follows from this model.

Owned tests compare literal five-stage tables, actual arithmetic, stack timing,
retained/refetched words, checkpoint continuation and transactional failures.
The optional original test adds a fresh independent RAM/PMCTL fixture for each
existing direct-stream invocation. The later record-stream serial cadence does
not drive this separate initialization fixture. It boots the unchanged serial
kernel and executes all six PLL wait loops (16/4096/16/4096/16/16 NOPs) through
the bound sequencer:8291 actual instructions/8302 granted fetch clocks
reach8c0b2, with frame108c008 and final PMCTL40012. The stable/settled CLK_CFG0
candidate counts one core-clock lower bound per granted non-PMCTL-access clock,
including bubbles; only bypass intervals count CLKIN. Ideal ratios are18/9/36:5.
This does not qualify the physical IOP bridge, mux phase, absolute frequencies
or board scheduling. Full record-stream checks still use a separate fresh
functional CPU/device fixture and retain the8c08e frontier. Primary provenance
remains reference/sharc.json. Manufacturer words and private
assembler/decoder/implementation are not build or publication inputs.
