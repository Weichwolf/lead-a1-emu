Fetched arithmetic loops with internal PM conflicts

The bound sequencer composes a live native arithmetic frame with the existing
internal conflict-cache transport. A PM instruction issues its real Address
request and executes its real data access. On the cache-miss partial stall,
Address advances to Execute while F1/F2/Decode and the next cursor hold.
The arithmetic comparator does not sample the held F1 again. No branch mask,
loop pass count or instruction retirement supplies the missing clock.

Advancing clocks retain the ordinary end/start+1 comparisons and old-flag
sampling. Known cache hits and fills may finish through the existing checked
arithmetic drain; speculative F1/F2 words are still discarded at Decode.
Termination coincident with a new cache miss remains guarded: its stalled
drain/flush ordering needs a separate reference. Deferred/suspended cache
contexts, other simultaneous stalls and branch/cache compositions remain
bounded by their existing guards. No generic stall-free loop summary is used.

Owned tests check all20 E/A/D/F2/F1 entries of PGR Table4-31 columns2..5,
including the actual six-instruction wrap, real PM(PX) payload and later hits.
A conditional producer sets AZ in Execute just before F1=start+1 is held.
The independent expectation is two complete passes and stores, with R5=-1;
sampling the held address would terminate early. FOREVER retains unresolved
arithmetic flags. Disabled/frozen misses preserve cache contents/knowledge
and continue real stores without hits. A prefilled frozen hit has a cached NOP
against a different RAM decrement: exactly the first unconflicted RAM word
decrements R7, while later conflict hits execute the retained cached word.
Forty-eight occupied/retired-Execute checkpoints restore separate
RAM/core/cache/transport and verify payloads.
A new-miss termination rejection preserves CPU/cache/transport and prior RAM
stores; it is an explicit remaining gap, not successful termination.

The optional original continuation now retires its actual foreground NOP
and dual PM/SYSCTL instruction. RAM at8c004 equals the prepared R11 patch
shifted to its documented48-bit store position. The saved vector and PX stay
unchanged. The next quiet clock fails at the existing SYSCTL Decode/Address
width guard, with read4/effective204/pending4 and the old end word still
captured in F2 while Decode holds the NOP. The failed clock preserves native,
cache, transport and control state and the earlier committed RAM patch.
Executing that rewritten word, restoring the saved vector, leaving the loader
and application boot remain
unqualified. The ordinary109318-cell oracle per cadence remains independent;
instruction-only streams still stop at8c08e.

Primary constraints: PGRrev2.4 4-5/8,4-53/58,4-79/85 Tables4-31/33,A-32/45;
native arithmetic/cache contracts and pinned provenance in reference/sharc.json.
These are explicitly granted internal transport clocks. No vendor simulator,
silicon timing, physical Nord CCLK or complete board schedule is qualified.
